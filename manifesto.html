Big Ideas in Biology

A running notebook of problems, ideas, and ambitions in biology that I think are exceptionally interesting — and that I hope someone reading this will actually work on. Biotechnology is not just about therapeutics. There are extraordinary problems to be solved at every scale, from molecules to ecosystems, and this guide is meant especially for students trying to figure out what to do next.

Version 1.0 is ten ideas per category. Cards are living documents and will be revised, expanded, and added to over time. You can filter by category or scroll the full list.
