Fast Biology Bounties: A Quick Update

April 27, 2026 shortform

A few weeks ago I announced the Fast Biology Bounties — a $10,000 prize pool for ideas that could make experiments run faster. We received over 200 submissions in ten days, which was more than I expected.The submissions came from 34 countries. The plurality were from graduate students, followed by postdocs and independent researchers.

The winning idea, from a graduate student in Amsterdam, proposed using pre-warmed media to shave twenty minutes off every overnight growth curve. Simple, cheap, and almost certainly right. That's exactly the kind of thing we were looking for: the sort of trick experienced scientists know but never write down.

The second-place submission suggested a minor modification to a standard transformation protocol that cuts hands-on time by half.Specifically: pre-mixing the competent cells with recovery media before adding DNA, so the heat shock and recovery happen in a single tube without a transfer step. Again: not glamorous, not publishable, but genuinely useful.

We'll run another round in the fall. If you have an idea — even a half-formed one — send it to me.
